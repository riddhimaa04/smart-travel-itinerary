How the match score works

Every destination starts at 0 points and earns points from four simple rules. The total is out of 100, and that total is the match percentage.

Please note: this is a rule-based prototype using sample data. It is not machine learning, it does not use ChatGPT or any AI service, and it does not use live prices. Destinations scoring below 50% are not shown.
